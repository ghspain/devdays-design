# Local logo assets

Logos are stored locally so canvas exports do not depend on third-party CORS
headers or URLs that may disappear.

- Celonis: official website wordmark, converted into black and white SVG variants.
- GitHub Community Spain: official community lockups from `ghspain/ghspain.github.io`.
- TechRiders: transparent community logo from `techriders.tajamar.es`, wrapped as
  black and white SVG variants.

Use the black variant on light artwork and the white variant on dark artwork.
